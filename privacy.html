2. Contact form

What I collect: your name, company (optional), work email address and the message you write. The form also sends technical data that is needed to deliver it, such as your IP address and browser details.

Why: to read your enquiry and reply to it. Where your enquiry leads to an engagement, I also use the data to prepare and carry out that engagement.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps taken at your request before entering into a contract) and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in replying to and keeping a record of business enquiries).

How it is sent: the form is relayed through Web3Forms, which forwards the message to my email inbox and processes it on my behalf under a data processing agreement (web3forms.com/dpa). For transfers from the European Economic Area, that agreement relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Decision 2021/914). Web3Forms may be located outside the EEA.

How long I keep it: if an enquiry does not lead to work, I delete the message and my reply within 12 months. If it leads to a contract, I keep the records for 7 years, as Dutch tax law requires for business records (Article 52 of the Dutch General Tax Act, AWR). Web3Forms may keep its own copy of each submission for the period set in its data processing agreement.

3. Booking a call

The "Book a 30-min intro call" buttons open a booking page hosted by Calendly. Calendly collects the name, email address and any details you enter there, and it sets its own cookies on its website. Those cookies are governed by Calendly's own privacy policy and cookie settings, not by this site. I see the name, email and topic you give when you book, and I use them only to hold the call and follow up on it.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps at your request) and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (organising business calls). Calendly may transfer data outside the European Economic Area. It relies on the safeguards described in its own privacy policy.

How long I keep it: the same periods as in section 2.

4. Email and direct contact

If you email me directly, or click the email address or the LinkedIn link, I process the content of your message and your address to reply. My email is provided by Google (Gmail). Google handles those emails under its own terms as an independent provider. I use the same retention periods as in section 2.

5. Website analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages are visited and how many people visit them. It is cookieless. It does not place a tracking identifier on your device, and I see only aggregated statistics, not information about individual visitors. The conversion markers record only that a visitor pressed a contact button. They contain no personal data.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used). Because the analytics do not store or read information on your device, no consent banner is needed under the Dutch Telecommunications Act (Article 11.7a).

6. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical data such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the site. Read GitHub's privacy statement. I do not have access to these logs.

7. Fonts and third parties on this site

The typeface used on this site is downloaded from the same server as the site itself. Your browser does not contact Google or any other font provider when you visit.

8. Cookies

This website sets no cookies of its own, and its analytics are cookieless. Cookies may be set on Calendly's website if you choose to book a call there, as explained in section 3.

9. Who receives your data

  • Web3Forms — relays contact form messages, as a processor under its data processing agreement. Transfers from the EEA rely on EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Calendly — hosts call bookings, as a processor. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum.
  • Cloudflare — aggregated, cookieless website analytics, as a processor under its data processing terms.
  • GitHub — hosts this website, under its privacy statement and data processing terms.
  • Google — provides my email (Gmail), as an independent provider under its own terms.

I do not sell your data and do not share it for marketing. Some of these providers are located in the United States or elsewhere outside the EEA. Each provider's transfer safeguard is listed above.

10. Your rights

  • access the personal data I hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased, where the law allows;
  • restrict or object to my processing, including processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where I rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email rscloudsolutions@gmail.com. I will respond within one month. If you are not satisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

No internet transmission is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive personal data, such as health or payroll records for identifiable employees, through the contact form. Share such material only after we agree a secure method.
